Account Data Deletion Request

To request the deletion of your personal data associated with any VengalaTech application, please follow the instructions below.

How to Submit a Request

1

Send your deletion request via email to our dedicated support address:

vsrinu26f@gmail.com
2

Include all the following information in your email to help us identify your account:

Required Information

  • App Name: The specific name of the VengalaTech application
  • User ID / Username: The identifier you use within the app
  • Registered Email Address: The email address associated with your account
  • Your Request: A clear statement that you wish to delete your account and associated data

Contact Information

Please also provide a reliable email address where we can contact you if we need any further information to verify your identity and process your request.

Processing Time & Follow-Up

  • We will acknowledge and begin processing your request as soon as possible.
  • We will delete all your account information from our side when we process account deletion request. Underlying technologies may actually delete based on their working or their own retention policies.
  • Please be patient regarding response times, as it may take us a few business days to investigate and action your request.
  • If you have not received a response within one month, please resend your email. It is possible your initial request was missed or filtered as spam.
